Call us

Call (973) 488-5410 for emergencies and for scheduling. Dispatch answers day and night, every day of the year. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

The email for non-urgent questions is contact@expressplumbermontclairnj.com. We do not use online request forms, because calling gets a plumber moving faster.

What to tell dispatch

The more specific you are, the better the first visit goes. A short, clear description lets us send the right equipment.

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • What is happening: leak, backup, no hot water or something else.
  • Whether you have shut off the water and whether anyone is in danger.
  • Any pictures or details that help describe the problem when the plumber arrives.

Emergencies, appointments and questions

For burst pipes, sewer backups, gas smells near a water heater or no water at all, call right away. The line is open all day and night. For scheduled work such as a fixture swap or a boiler checkup, call during the day and ask for the earliest slot.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility first. Then call us once you are safe.
