Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026.

What we collect

When you call us, we may collect your phone number, your name and address, and the details of the plumbing problem you describe. If you email us, we collect your email address and the content of your message. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Our website does not use contact forms. The site is a static site, and we do not run accounts or sell products online.

How we use it

We use this information to dispatch plumbers, schedule and carry out work, answer questions and improve our service. We may keep records of work performed for warranty, billing and legal purposes.

Third party services

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and may show embedded Google Maps. These services may receive your IP address and browser information when a page loads, under their own privacy policies. We do not control those services.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with suppliers, insurers or authorities when needed to carry out work or meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you or ask us to delete it, subject to legal and record keeping requirements. Contact us at contact@expressplumbermontclairnj.com or call (973) 488-5410.

Retention and security

We keep call records and any information you give us only as long as needed to provide service, handle disputes and meet legal duties. We use reasonable safeguards to protect them, though no system is perfectly secure.

If you want us to review or delete information we hold about you, call or email us and we will tell you what we can do.

Children and contact

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@expressplumbermontclairnj.com or by phone at (973) 488-5410.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
