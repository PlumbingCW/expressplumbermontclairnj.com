Backups that call for jetting right away

A main line that backs up again a few weeks after a cable cleaning usually has heavy buildup on the walls. The cable punched a hole through the blockage but left the pipe coated. A backup in a restaurant or a multi-unit building is especially urgent.

If water is rising in a floor drain or tub, stop using water and call (973) 488-5410. We will decide whether to open the line first and jet it afterward.

How hydro jetting works

A hose with a nozzle goes into the line through a cleanout. Jets of water point backward and forward as the nozzle travels, scouring the walls and washing debris downstream. The pressure removes grease and scale that a cable leaves behind.

Because the cleaning happens against the entire pipe surface, jetting suits lines that clog repeatedly. It also helps clear tree roots when used with a cutting nozzle.

  1. Camera check. We look first to confirm the pipe is sound enough for jetting.
  2. Access. We use a cleanout or open a trap to feed the hose into the line.
  3. Jet the line. The nozzle travels the run, cleaning as it goes.
  4. Confirm. We camera the line again or run water to verify the result.

Jetting compared with cable cleaning

Cable cleaning is often the right first step. Jetting is the step up when the line is coated and keeps clogging.

What it doesScours the entire pipe wallCuts a hole through the blockage

When jetting is not the right tool

Pressure can make a damaged pipe worse. A cracked, collapsed or heavily offset line needs repair before cleaning. For that reason we camera the line before jetting whenever the history suggests damage.

If the pipe is broken, we explain the next step, which may be sewer line repair. If the camera shows a clean, solid pipe with buildup, jetting is a good choice.

What affects the price of hydro jetting

Jetting uses specialized equipment and takes more time than a standard cleaning.

  • Length and diameter of the line.

  • Amount and type of buildup.

Why older Montclair lines benefit

Older cast iron lines under Montclair houses develop rough scale on the walls, and that scale catches grease and paper. A line that looks fine on a camera may still have a rough lining that jetting can smooth by removing loose scale. That reduces repeat clogs on lines that are otherwise sound.

Restaurants and food businesses along Bloomfield Avenue and near Lackawanna Plaza face grease buildup as well, which makes jetting part of regular maintenance. Our commercial plumbing page covers scheduled service.

Codes and standards behind the work

Hydro jetting is used with the cleanouts that the International Plumbing Code requires, and it is not suited to pipe that is already cracked or collapsed.
