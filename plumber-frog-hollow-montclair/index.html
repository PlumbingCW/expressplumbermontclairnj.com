Frog Hollow at a glance

Montclair describes Frog Hollow as a business district along Valley Road, with Edgemont Memorial Park nearby. A widely used reference on Montclair notes that Valley Road is known locally as Frog Hollow. One real estate guide describes the surrounding blocks as mostly single-family homes first built in the 1920s on former marshland, covering about nine blocks near Bloomfield Avenue.

That guide is a single source, so we treat its description as a general pointer and not as a measured fact about your house. When you call, tell dispatch whether you are on Valley Road itself or on a side street, because the two can mean a business or a house.

Basements, sump pumps and water

A neighborhood description that mentions former marshland naturally raises a question about basement water. We do not have a measurement of groundwater on any particular Frog Hollow block, and we do not claim your basement is wet or dry. What we can do is look at yours.

If you have a sump pump, test it before the wet season by pouring water into the pit and watching it start. A pump that has not run for months can stick.

Before a plumber arrives

If water is near electrical equipment, stay out and call for help.

  • Switch off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel safely and dry.
  • Move boxes and electronics off the floor.
  • Note where the water enters and how fast it rises.
  • Do not run laundry or dishwashers while a floor drain is backing up.

Local rules to know

Sump pump discharge has to go somewhere permitted, and in Montclair the sewer system is run by the township Sewer Utility. Pumping groundwater into a sanitary sewer is generally restricted, so ask before you connect a discharge line.

A plumber can tell you what is allowed at your address and what a permit would cover.

Codes and standards behind the work

Plumbing repairs and replacements in Montclair follow the International Plumbing Code as adopted under the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code, and permits are handled by the township construction office.

We could not confirm the exact boundaries of Frog Hollow, which sources describe by Valley Road and the nearby park. We found no official flood or groundwater data for the neighborhood, so we do not state a risk. The marshland note comes from one real estate guide.
