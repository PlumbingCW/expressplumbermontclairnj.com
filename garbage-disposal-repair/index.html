When a disposal problem cannot wait

Water leaking from under the sink is the main urgent case. Shut off the supply stops under the sink or the main, and place a bucket under the leak. A disposal that is humming but not turning has a jam, and running it longer risks burning the motor.

Never put your hand in the disposal. Switch off power at the wall or the breaker, then call (973) 488-5410.

Why disposals jam, hum or leak

Many jams clear in a few minutes. Leaks from the housing mean the unit is finished.

SymptomCauseFix
Hums, does not spinJammed flywheelFree the jam with a hex key or tool
Nothing happensTripped reset button or no powerReset or restore power
Leaks from the bottomFailed seal or cracked housingUsually replace
Leaks from the topLoose sink flange or gasketReseat the flange
Slow drain beyond the disposalClog in the trap or branchClear the drain

Repair or replace a disposal

If the unit is jammed, tripping the breaker or leaking from the flange, repair is often practical. A unit leaking from the housing is done. Disposals tend to last as long as the motor and seals hold, and heavy use shortens that.

We give you both options when the choice is close, and we replace units at the sink with the right mount and electrical connection.

What a disposal visit looks like

  1. Power off. We switch off power and confirm it is off.
  2. Diagnose. We check the jam, the reset, the mounting and the drain.
  3. Repair or replace. We free the jam, reseat the flange or install a new unit.
  4. Test. We run water and the disposal to confirm there are no leaks.

What affects the price of disposal work

A jam release and a replacement are different jobs.

  • Whether the unit needs replacing.

  • Electrical connection or a new outlet.

Insurance generally does not cover disposal repair. A sudden leak that damages cabinets may be treated differently. Call (973) 488-5410 and we will give you the price before we start.

What not to put down the disposal

Grease, coffee grounds, pasta, rice and fibrous food like celery cause blockages beyond the disposal. A disposal grinds food, but it does not make grease flow. Cold water while running helps move waste, and a short run afterward clears the blades.

  • Grease and oil.
  • Bones, fruit pits and fibrous peels.
  • Coffee grounds, eggshells and pasta in quantity.
  • Non food items such as bottle caps.

Disposals in older Montclair kitchens

Many older Montclair kitchens have drain lines that run a long way to the stack, and some have galvanized or cast iron pipes. These lines are sensitive to grease buildup. A disposal adds food waste to a line that may already be narrowing, which is why we look at the drain as well as the unit.

If the kitchen sink backs up repeatedly, drain cleaning may be the real fix. We find out which problem you have before selling a new unit.

Codes and standards behind the work

A disposal wired to a household circuit falls under the National Electrical Code (NFPA 70), and a unit with a dishwasher connection must have its drain routed with an air gap or high loop as local code requires.
