Upper Montclair at a glance

Upper Montclair is part of Montclair Township, not a separate town, even though it is often mistaken for one. Its ZIP code is 07043. The area began as a rural community called Speertown, named for an early Dutch settler, and Montclair Township itself was incorporated in 1868.

For plumbing, the useful point is that you call the same township offices for permits and the same water and sewer departments as the rest of Montclair.

Part of Montclair

Permits, water and sewer questions go through Montclair Township, the same as the rest of the town.

ZIP code 07043

Upper Montclair uses 07043. Most of the rest of Montclair uses 07042.

A village business district

Valley Road and Bellevue Avenue form the commercial center, near the Upper Montclair train station.

Parks close by

Anderson Park, Yantacaw Brook Park and Mountainside Park are all in or beside the neighborhood.

Homes and shops around the village center

The township has designated an Upper Montclair Business Historic District with 93 historic structures built from the 1880s to the 1930s. Valley Road and Bellevue Avenue are noted for Tudor Revival buildings, and most are one or two stories with retail on the ground floor.

That mix matters for plumbing because a call here may be a house, a shop or a building that is both. Restaurants and cafes bring grease and drain questions, while homes bring the usual water heater, fixture and drain calls.

Practical steps before a plumber arrives

Find your main water shutoff before you need it. In many homes it is near where the water line enters the house, often in the basement or a utility room. If water is spreading, close it and call.

For a drain problem, stop running water into the affected fixtures and avoid pouring chemical cleaners down the drain. Chemicals can splash and complicate the work that follows.

  • Know where the main shutoff is and that it turns.
  • Note which fixtures are slow and which still work.
  • Take photos of any water damage for your insurer.
  • Keep your street address and a cross street ready for dispatch.

Water, sewer and permits in Montclair

Montclair has a township Water Bureau and Sewer Utility, and the sewer connection between your house and the street can involve township rules. Before a repair on the line to the street, ask what part belongs to the township and what part is yours.

Many plumbing repairs and replacements need a permit under New Jersey rules, and the township construction office handles them. Dispatch can explain what a given job usually involves.

Codes and standards behind the work

Plumbing repairs and replacements in Montclair follow the International Plumbing Code as adopted under the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code, and permits are handled by the township construction office.
