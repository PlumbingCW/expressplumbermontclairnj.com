Main line blockage, first steps

A main line blockage affects more than one fixture. The basement floor drain may fill, toilets may bubble and tubs may back up with water from the washer. Do not run water or flush, because everything you add goes to the same blocked pipe.

Move valuables off the floor in the lowest part of the house and call (973) 488-5410. We will send a plumber with a main line machine.

What causes a blocked sewer line

Roots, grease, paper products and wipes top the list. Roots enter at joints and spread into a mass that catches everything else. Grease cools in the line and hardens, narrowing the pipe a little with each use.

Wipes labeled flushable do not break down like toilet paper and tangle with roots and grease. Sometimes the cause is structural, such as a belly or a broken section, and cleaning only treats the symptom.

How we clean a sewer line

A cable machine opens the line quickly. For lines coated with grease or scale, hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall more thoroughly.

  1. Locate the cleanout. We start at the outside cleanout when there is one.
  2. Run the machine. A heavy cable with a cutting head opens the blockage.
  3. Clear roots or buildup. Roots are cut back and debris is flushed forward.
  4. Verify flow. We run water, and recommend a camera if the line is unclear.

Roots and trees in Montclair

Montclair is known for its tree lined streets, and many of the trees are decades old. Their roots reach lines when joints open, which is common in clay and older cast iron. If you have a mature tree near the line from the house to the street, cleaning every so often may be needed until the joint is repaired.

We explain the choice plainly. Cutting roots clears the line today, and repairing the joint stops them from coming back.

Codes and standards behind the work

Main drain cleanouts are required by the International Plumbing Code, and a line that cannot be cleared through one is a sign that it needs inspection.
