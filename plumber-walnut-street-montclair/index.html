Walnut Street at a glance

The township lists the Walnut-Grove business district along Walnut Street, extending to Grove Street. The Montclair Farmer's Market operates in the Walnut Street train station parking lot. A local real estate guide says Walnut Street crosses most of Montclair and ends at the Glen Ridge border, and it describes single-family homes and condos along it.

Because it is a long street, the name alone does not tell us where you are. Your street number and cross street do. If you are near the Glen Ridge line, dispatch will confirm which town you are in before sending anyone, since the two have different permit offices.

A business district

Walnut-Grove is one of the township's six business districts.

Near the train station

The Walnut Street station and its farmers market anchor the district.

Toward Glen Ridge

One guide says the street ends at the Glen Ridge border, so some calls fall just across the line.

Homes and businesses

Houses, condos and small businesses share the corridor.

Shops, restaurants and homes along one corridor

A business district means some calls come from food businesses and shops, not just homes. Restaurants and cafes depend on working drains, grease control and dependable hot water, and a stoppage during service hours is costly.

Homes on the surrounding streets bring the usual water heater, fixture and drain calls. We treat each call by what the building is, not by the street name.

Before a plumber arrives

In a building with several occupants, tell the owner or manager early so access and shutoffs are arranged in advance.

  • Know where the main shutoff is and whether other tenants share it.
  • Tell staff or tenants not to use the affected fixtures.
  • Make sure the plumber can reach the work area during business hours.
  • Take photos of any water damage for the owner and the insurer.

Local rules to know

Walnut Street crosses into Glen Ridge, which is a separate borough. Permits for work on the Glen Ridge side go through that borough, and our Glen Ridge page explains how we handle calls there. In Montclair, the township construction office handles permits, and the township Water Bureau and Sewer Utility handle the utility side.

Grease and backflow rules for food businesses are set locally, so ask the township which apply to your building.

Codes and standards behind the work

Plumbing repairs and replacements in Montclair follow the International Plumbing Code as adopted under the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code, and permits are handled by the township construction office.
