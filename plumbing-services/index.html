Emergency services and scheduled work

Some problems need a plumber now. Burst pipes, sewage backups, leaking tanks and flooding sump pits belong in that group, and our line answers day and night for them. Other work, such as a new water heater or a bathroom remodel, is better planned.

Both kinds of work follow the same rule. You hear the price before work starts and you approve it.

How we decide what to recommend

Many plumbing problems have more than one fix. We start with the least expensive option that will actually solve the problem, and we explain when it will not. A cable cleaning may be enough for a one time clog, while a repeating blockage calls for a camera and sometimes hydro jetting.

The same logic applies to water heaters. A thermostat is a repair, while a leaking tank is a replacement, and we say which one you have.

How to choose the right service

If water is spreading, start with burst pipe repair or leak detection. If a drain is slow or backing up, drain cleaning is the usual first step. If hot water is the issue, our water heater pages explain repair, replacement and installation.

Not sure where to start? Call (973) 488-5410 and describe what you see and hear. We will point you to the right service and say what to do while you wait.

Codes and standards behind the work

Every service here is carried out to the International Plumbing Code, with permits pulled under the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code where they are required.
