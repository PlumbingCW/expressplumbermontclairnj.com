Montclair homes and their plumbing

Montclair has a lot of housing from the early 1900s, from large houses in Upper Montclair to compact homes around the South End and Frog Hollow. Older houses often have cast iron drains, galvanized supply pipe and boilers that were fitted long before modern code. These materials fail in predictable ways, which helps us find problems quickly.

Tree lined streets near Watchung Plaza and Mountainside Park bring roots into old sewer lines, and basements across the area are used to storm water. When Hurricane Ida passed through in September 2021, basements across northern New Jersey took on water. A sump pump with a backup and a clear sewer line are the practical defenses, which is why sump pump service and sewer line repair are among our busiest calls.

Drains, heaters and hidden leaks cover most calls

Most of what we see falls into three groups. Drains and sewer lines clog or fail, water heaters stop or leak, and pipes leak where nobody can see them. Each one has a different urgency and a different fix.

If you are not sure which group your problem is in, call (973) 488-5410. Describe what you see and hear, and we will tell you whether to shut something off, wait for a crew or schedule a visit. Our pages on drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection explain each one in more detail.
