Planning around business hours

A shop that loses water during the lunch rush loses money fast. We schedule non emergency work for quiet hours when we can and tell you in advance what will be shut off and for how long. For emergencies, call (973) 488-5410 and we will say when a plumber can reach you.

Property managers can give us a list of units, shutoff locations and past problems. That saves time on every visit and cuts down on guesswork.

Grease, drains and backflow

Restaurants and food shops rely on grease traps and interceptors that need regular cleaning. A full trap backs up into the kitchen and can trigger a health inspection. Backflow preventers on water lines also need testing on a schedule set by the local water authority.

Ask us what your building has and when each device was last serviced. Records help during an inspection and make budgeting easier.

Codes and standards behind the work

Commercial work is governed by the International Plumbing Code as adopted under the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code, and backflow protection, grease interceptors and fixture counts are checked at inspection.

Safety steps while you wait for a plumber

These steps limit damage until a technician arrives. Call (973) 488-5410 and tell us what you can see.

  • Shut off the main water valve if water is spreading, and keep staff away from wet floors.
  • Do not touch electrical equipment standing in water.
  • Move stock and records to higher ground and take photos for insurance.
  • Do not pour chemicals into a backed up drain.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility first.
