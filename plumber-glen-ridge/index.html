Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak

What you do in the first few minutes decides how much damage the house takes. These four steps take less than five minutes and protect floors, walls and electrical equipment.

Step 1

Shut off the main water valve.

In most Glen Ridge basements it sits where the water line enters, near the meter. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

Step 2

Kill power in wet areas.

If water is near outlets, switch off only the affected breakers and only if you can reach the panel dry.

Step 3

Clear the area.

Lift rugs, boxes and electronics off the floor, and move people and pets away from the water.

Why older Glen Ridge homes call for a plumber

Older houses were plumbed for a different era, and the pipe material differs from house to house. Depending on the age of the house, drains, sewer laterals and supply lines may be cast iron, clay, galvanized steel, copper or plastic, and a plumber needs to see yours before saying which.

Because the borough is tree lined, roots also reach old sewer lines. A basement floor drain that backs up after heavy rain is often a root or joint problem on the line to the street. We run a camera to find the cause, and we explain whether the fix is a cleaning or a repair.
