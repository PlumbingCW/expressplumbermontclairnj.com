Watchung Plaza at a glance

The township has designated the Watchung Plaza Historic District, which covers 24 historic structures built from the 1900s to the 1930s. The district describes an early twentieth century shopping center serving the neighborhood around the Watchung Avenue railroad station. Watchung Plaza Park dates to 1918 and Watchung Park to 1920.

The surrounding streets are residential, and the plaza itself is mostly one and two story buildings. Tell dispatch whether your call is for a plaza business or a nearby house, because the access and the equipment can be different.

Older buildings and historic district rules

Building age is a reason to look carefully before a repair. It does not tell us what is behind the walls, so we do not assume a pipe type. A plumber has to inspect the work area first.

Inside a locally designated historic district, changes to the outside of a building can be subject to township review. Roof vents, exterior equipment and new penetrations are the kinds of work worth checking with the township Historic Preservation office before you schedule.

Steps that protect an older building

Forcing a stuck valve is the most common way a small problem becomes a flood, so call first if a valve will not move.

  • Learn where the main shutoff is, and test that it turns.
  • Do not force an old valve, since it may break.
  • Keep a record of repairs and the dates for future owners.
  • Ask what a repair will do to plaster, tile and trim before work starts.

Local rules to know

Montclair Township runs a Water Bureau and Sewer Utility, and permits for plumbing replacements go through the township construction office. Historic preservation staff handle questions about designated districts.

Ask dispatch which of the three is likely involved before a larger job begins.

Codes and standards behind the work

Plumbing repairs and replacements in Montclair follow the International Plumbing Code as adopted under the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code, and permits are handled by the township construction office.
