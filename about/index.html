What we do

We repair and install plumbing in homes and small businesses in Montclair and the neighboring towns. Our work covers drains and sewer lines, water heaters, leaks, burst pipes, sump pumps and fixtures. The full list is on our plumbing services page.

We also answer an emergency line around the clock, because the problems that cost the most are the ones that happen at night.

How we work

Price before work starts

We explain the fix and the cost, and you approve it before we begin.

Explain, then fix

We tell you what caused the problem, so you can decide about follow-up work.

Least expensive fix first

We start with the option that will actually solve the problem, not the biggest ticket.

Tell you when to wait

If a problem can wait for a regular appointment, we say so.

Checking credentials

Plumbing work in New Jersey is done by licensed plumbers. If you want to confirm credentials before work starts, ask dispatch and check the license of the plumber through the state.

You can also ask dispatch what the visit will involve before any work begins.

Our connection to Montclair

We focus on Upper Montclair, Montclair Center, the South End and the towns beside them. Older houses, boilers and basements are common service topics here, and a clear description of the symptom helps us point you to the right next step.

We also serve Glen Ridge, Verona, Cedar Grove and Bloomfield. See the service area page for details.

Why homeowners call a local team

Older houses hide surprises behind plaster and under slabs. A plumber who works in the same few towns every week learns which streets have clay sewer laterals, which blocks have shared lines and which neighborhoods see basement water after hard rain. That local picture shortens diagnosis and keeps repairs honest.

We answer the phone ourselves, take notes on every call and send the right technician with the right parts when we can. If the job needs a specialist or a permit, we say so before we start.

The services we are asked for most

Most calls fall into a few groups: clogged drains and sewer backups, leaks and burst pipes, water heater trouble and fixture repairs. Seasonal calls follow the weather, with frozen pipes in winter and sump pumps in spring and fall storms.

See our plumbing services for the full list, or call (973) 488-5410 to describe what is happening. We will tell you what to do while you wait.

Pricing and communication

You get a clear price before work starts. If we find something else during the job, we stop, show you and explain the options. You decide what happens next, and nothing is added without your say.

After the work, we explain what we did and what to watch for. Keep that note for your records and your insurer.

Emergency and scheduled work

Emergency calls come first, and we tell you honestly how soon a plumber can arrive. Scheduled work such as a fixture swap or a water heater replacement is booked for a time that suits you. Either way, call (973) 488-5410 and we will talk it through.

What you can expect when we arrive

A technician will tell you what they plan to check and give a clear price before work starts. We protect floors, clean up and show you the problem when we can. If the job needs more than expected, we stop and explain before going further.

We take notes on what we found so you have a record for your insurer or your next plumber.

Codes and standards behind the work

We work to the International Plumbing Code as adopted under the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code.

How the emergency line works

When you call (973) 488-5410, dispatch asks what is happening, where you are and whether anyone is in danger. We tell you what to shut off while a plumber is on the way. Then the plumber arrives, assesses the problem and explains the price before starting.
